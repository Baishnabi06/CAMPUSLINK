How is my score calculated?

Your score is out of 100. It adds up six parts, each worth the maximum shown in the breakdown above.

  • Academics: your CGPA and whether you have any backlogs.
  • Skills: how many skills you list, and how many of them recruiters are asking for in open drives.
  • Projects and certifications: the more you add, up to a point, the more you score.
  • Profile and resume: an uploaded resume and a completed profile.
  • Activity: the drives you apply to and the interviews you attend.

The score updates as soon as your profile or activity changes. It shows how strong your profile is today. It is not a prediction of whether you will be placed.
